Local-First Privacy Guarantee

All detection and redaction processes run 100% locally on your computer inside your browser. SafeShield never stores, transmits, or logs your original prompt text or detected personal data. Only anonymous aggregate counts (e.g. 5 phones redacted) are persisted locally in chrome.storage.local. Temporary placeholder mappings live exclusively in ephemeral content script memory and are discarded when you close or leave the tab.
